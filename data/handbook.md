# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook (sample)

## Vacation and time off
New employees receive 15 paid vacation days per year, accrued monthly.
After three years of service this increases to 20 days per year.
Unused vacation days, up to a maximum of 5, carry over to the next calendar year.
Employees also receive 10 company holidays and 5 paid sick days per year.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.
The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500.

## Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal.
Meals during travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days with itemized receipts.

## Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences.
Requests above $500 need manager approval before purchase.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every three years.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave.
